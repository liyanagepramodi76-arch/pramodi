# 🌱 Rubora – Smart Rubber Price Matching and Farmer-Buyer Connection Platform

**Connecting Sri Lankan Rubber Farmers with Better Buyers Through Technology**

## 📌 About the Project

Rubora is a web-based Smart Rubber Marketplace designed to connect rubber farmers with commercial rubber buyers in Sri Lanka.

The platform aims to improve the traditional rubber trading process by providing a centralized digital environment where farmers can publish their available rubber stocks, buyers can publish their purchasing requirements, and both parties can discover suitable trading opportunities.

Rubora promotes transparent pricing, efficient communication, and smarter decision-making in the rubber industry.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Connect rubber farmers directly with commercial buyers.
- Provide a centralized platform for rubber stock and buying requirements.
- Improve price transparency through price comparison.
- Identify potential farmer-buyer matches automatically.
- Simplify trade request management.
- Support farmers and buyers with a Dry Rubber Content (DRC) calculator.

## 💡 Price Matching System

Rubora identifies potential trading opportunities by comparing farmer selling prices with buyer purchasing prices.

**Main Matching Rule:**

`Buyer Buying Price >= Farmer Selling Price`

The system also considers other relevant factors such as:
- Rubber type.
- Required quantity.
- Location.
- Stock availability.

This helps farmers and buyers identify suitable trading opportunities more efficiently.

## 🧮 Dry Rubber Content Calculator

Rubora includes a Dry Rubber calculation feature to help users estimate the dry rubber weight based on rubber weight and relevant DRC or metrolac measurements.
